# Studigo launch film

`studigo-launch-film-34s-1080p.mp4`: 34.5 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC. The narrated cut from Round 3 of the
launch film (2026-10-08): voice is Benji (see `assets/voice/studigo-benji/`), generated dragon footage, code-built
UI scenes, instrumental music and synthesized effects.

This is a 1080p re-encode (CRF 20) of the master render, kept small for the repo. The film's Remotion project
and scripts are in `design/launch-film/`. Voice, lip-sync and music were checked by measurement, not by ear.
