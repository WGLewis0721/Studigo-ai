07 / THE IMPORTANT BIT

Your class material stays the point.

Studigo is designed to help you work from the files you upload, not replace your teacher or quietly expand the assignment.

Will it make up answers?

When the material does not support an answer, Studigo says so. When it can answer, the page or section is part of the response.

Which file takes priority?

Your teacher's study guide is the map. Supporting class handouts, worksheets, slides, notes, and textbooks help explain it without silently changing what you need to study.

Are my uploads private?

Study material belongs to you. Files stay private to your account and are used to make your own Study Room useful.
