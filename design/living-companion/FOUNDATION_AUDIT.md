## 6. Layout and placement constraints

- **Floating dock:** four corners only (`Dock = br | bl | tr | tl`, `companion-logic.ts` L11). A drag ends at the nearest corner (`dockFromDrop`). There is no normalized free position. LC-5 needs a versioned `pos` with legacy migration.
- **Size:** base window 116×138 CSS px (`WINDOW`, L17). Scale range 0.75–2.0 (L21–22).
- **Avoidance:** `area()` bounds (`engine.ts` L127–155) and control rectangles (L162–168), with a 7.5% coverage tolerance (`COVERED_SHARE`, `companion-logic.ts` L64). Phase 3 non-chat rooms use `controlSafeDock` (`companion-logic.ts` L87–99), with 8px padding. It falls back to the header seat when no corner is clear.
- **Header strip (LC-6 host):** no strip slot exists. `StudigoRail` is aria-hidden, and under Phase 3 it is 7px high with children hidden (`phase3-foundation.css` L46–47). The strip's motion comes from `ModeFrame`. On desktop, after 3 s idle (timer at `mode-header.tsx` L155–167), it collapses (`data-collapsed`, `grid-template-rows: 0fr`, `globals.css` ~L3397–3412) and reopens on hover, focus, or click. Phones (≤700px) keep the controls full size.
- **Resolved 2026-11-15:** the owner confirmed the strip closes after 3 s idle. The docs that said "100%→60% tuck" are corrected: `RECONCILIATION.md` C7, the LC plan, `HAIKU_HANDOFF.md`, and pack docs 01, 06, and 09. My earlier audit wrongly listed `INTEGRATION_MAP.md` §6; it never had that wording. On `main` I found no 100%→60% scale rule (searched `scale(`, `60%`, and `tuck`). `globals.css` L4182 and L4246 still say "60% tuck" in comments, and `RestRegion` (`learn-panel.tsx` L53–55) is a plain wrapper. Those are app-code comments, so they are unchanged. The drawer to preserve is `ModeFrame`, whose 3 s timer is at `mode-header.tsx` L155–167.
- **Different behavior:** `prefs.tuck` fades the window to 28% opacity while the page scrolls (`companion.css` L33, `engine.ts` L599–606). It is unrelated to the Phase 3 drawer.
