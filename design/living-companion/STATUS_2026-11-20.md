## 2. The renderer as built

- **Not Rive.** `rig-renderer.ts` draws the approved front-facing dragon image on a deforming mesh (`lib/companion-motion/rig.ts`) with one WebGL call. The mesh moves head, frills, tail and body; a fragment shader moves the irises, lids, mouth and cheeks. Nothing is redrawn by hand, so identity stays on-model.
- Interface: `CompanionRenderer` in `components/companion/renderers.ts`, `kind: "sprite" | "rig"`. There is no `RiveRenderer`. LC-DEC-013 planned a lazy one behind the same seam.
- Fallbacks: flag off, `NEXT_PUBLIC_STUDIGO_COMPANION=off`, WebGL failure → sprite renderer.
- Not upgraded: the Home full-body stage and the header mascot are still static (LC-DEC-020, OPEN).
- Open question: LC-DEC-012 said "renderer choice proven by a ≤7-day measured spike". The spike evidence does not exist, so the choice of the mesh rig over Rive has not been formally made or approved. Treat it as a **PROPOSAL** until the owner decides (step 12).

## 3. Rive: what we know

Source: `evidence/rive-licensing-notes.md` (summarized fetches; confidence is medium; read the raw ToS before relying on it).

- Free tier can export `.riv` files, but **the output plays a Rive splash screen**. CLI builds on Free may carry a watermark; a clean CLI build needs a workspace on **Cadet or higher**.
- Cadet is the lowest plan without the splash. Prices recorded: Free $0, Cadet $9, Voyager $32, Enterprise $120, per seat (yearly/monthly toggle). Student plan is "not for commercial use".
- ToS D.4: you own your User Content. Whether an exported `.riv` counts as User Content, and an explicit grant to ship it commercially: **NOT FOUND**. ToS C.6 has a $10M revenue threshold.
- Account: Rive Free tier, CLI 1.9.2 installed on the owner's Windows PC, `rive doctor` passes (auth ok, live-link ok).
- **Do not adopt Rive in a shipped kids' app until the `.riv` terms are confirmed in writing** (plan H4, `evidence/LC-1.md`).
