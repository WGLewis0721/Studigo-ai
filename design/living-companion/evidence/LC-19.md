## Method notes

- Hops and spins are read from the rig mesh. The script wraps `WebGLRenderingContext.bufferSubData` in the test page (test-only, no product change) to capture the rig's 961-point vertex buffer. Hop is the mean bottom-row displacement times 0.28125 px per rig unit at default size. Spin is the sign of the bottom row's x-slope (negative means mirrored). The `.cmpRigBody` matrix is also read when a drawing shows.
- Drawn-body values from `getComputedStyle` are in local CSS px before the `.cmpBody` 0.75 scale ([companion.css:70](../../../apps/web/components/companion/companion.css)). Visual equals 0.75 times the reported value; 24 local px is 18 visual px, matching the rig.
- Threshold: hop under 4 px (card). Continuous motion is 0.2 px or 0.2 deg peak to peak or more. Normal runs show 18 px hops, spin flips, 3 to 5 px idle motion, and glances, so the instrument sees each effect.
- Head motion peak-to-peak includes gaze-driven pitch and yaw as well as breathing; the two are not separated.
- Idle glances are random, every 4.8 to 9 s ([engine.ts:888-898](../../../apps/web/components/companion/engine.ts)), so counts vary run to run. One run per condition and viewport.
- Vignettes do not show in the DOM (their poses are null or gaze looks). "No vignettes in Calm" is supported by code only ([director.ts:201](../../../apps/web/lib/companion-motion/director.ts)).
- No uncaught page errors and no console errors in any run.
- Dev-server artifact: at startup Next rewrote one heading in `apps/web/AGENTS.md`, which `results.json` lists under `productChangesVsSha`. It was reverted before the commit and does not affect runtime.
- [results.json](LC-19/results.json) is 0.9 MB because it keeps every trigger sample for audit.
