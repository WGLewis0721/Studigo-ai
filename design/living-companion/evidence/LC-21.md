## Method

- Rooms: `legacy` is `ui=legacy`; `Phase 3` is `ui=phase3`. The mode (quiz, coach, learn mission) is in the Room column.
- Viewports: desktop 1440x900 at device scale 1. Phone 390x844 at device scale 2, with `isMobile` and `hasTouch`. 200% zoom is emulated as 720x450 and 195x422 at device scale 2.
- Touch drags use CDP touch input. Mouse drags grab the grip. Keyboard checks focus the pet area and press arrow keys.
- The fixture is synthetic (`/dev/study`). The script writes only companion preferences, in the browser context it creates. No signed-in data, and no network beyond localhost.
- The desktop drawer folds after three seconds without pointer activity. Measurements that depend on the header hold the pointer on the rail.
- Screenshots are at CSS-pixel scale and clipped to the companion. V05 timings include screenshot latency, so they are approximate.
- A row can have one entry per viewport and room. OPEN means a person, a real device or a hardware rig is needed. The automated part of the verdict is in the note.

## Failures found

All seven are product findings. Expected and actual values come from the final run. Code references point to the tree at `537c037`.

### F1. Phase 3: arrow keys do not move him (UI01, desktop and phone)

- **Repro:** open `/dev/study?mode=quiz&ui=phase3`, focus the pet area (`.cmpHit`), press ArrowRight five times.
- **Expected:** about 16 px per press, as in the legacy room (desktop ArrowLeft moved him -80 px in five presses).
- **Actual:** the left edge stays at 170 px (desktop) and 26 px (phone) for all five presses. The saved position changes (desktop x 0.016, y 0.114; phone x 0.072, y 0) but the new spot is never shown.
- **Code:** [engine.ts](../../../apps/web/components/companion/engine.ts) line 305 uses a saved spot only outside Phase 3 (`prefs.pos && !safeRoom`). Lines 328 and 329 pick a safe corner inside Phase 3.

### F2. Phase 3: a dropped spot snaps to a corner (UI02, desktop and phone)

- **Repro:** Phase 3 quiz. Drag the frame by its grip to a free point in the middle of the page, then release.
- **Expected:** the frame stays where it was dropped (UI02, non-corner placement).
- **Actual:** desktop drops at centre (298, 359) and the frame ends at (228, 359), the left safe corner. Phone drops at (154, 354) and ends at (84, 354). The saved position is normalized and not a corner (desktop 0.07, 0.00; phone 0.32, 0.00), but it is not shown.
- **Code:** the same lines as F1. The comment just above line 305 says Phase 3 rooms keep their corners, which conflicts with the UI02 row. Owner decision: keep corner-only placement in Phase 3, or allow free spots there.

### F3. Phase 3 phone: after a minimize, the bring-back tab does not restore him while the page is scrolled (A01)

- **Repro:** phone 390x844, `/dev/study?mode=quiz&ui=phase3`. Scroll the page to about 865 px. Activate "Send Studigo back to his seat". Then activate "Bring Studigo back out".
- **Expected:** the tab brings him back. The tab handler's comment says an explicit request relaxes the no-overlap fallback.
- **Actual:** he stays hidden at window scroll 865 (`summon: {windowY: 865, hidden: true}`) and the tab stays visible.
- **Code:** [engine.ts](../../../apps/web/components/companion/engine.ts). The minimize handler (line 712) clears `userSummoned`. `autoSeated` is also false after a learner minimize, so the tab handler (line 718) sets `userSummoned = autoSeated`, which is false. `placeInRoom` then calls `controlSafeDock` (lines 328 and 329), finds no free corner at that scroll position, and seats him again. A seat from the automatic path sets `autoSeated`, which is why the tab did restore him in the same run after the Tab sweep (`restoredFromSeat: true`).

### F4. Phase 3 phone: Tab scrolls the page, he seats himself, and the pet area, menu and minimize drop out of the Tab order (A01)

- **Repro:** phone 390x844, Phase 3 quiz. Press Tab from the top of the page. He is visible through Tab 14. At Tab 15 the window scrolls to y 406 and he seats himself (hidden). The bring-back tab is the only companion control reached (press 25). The pet area, menu and minimize are never focused during the sweep.
- **Expected:** while he is visible, the pet area, menu and minimize are reachable by Tab, as on desktop, where all four are reached within the first 30 presses.
- **Actual:** the scroll seats him, so those three controls are unreachable by keyboard.
- **Code:** [engine.ts](../../../apps/web/components/companion/engine.ts) lines 328 to 333. With no free corner he is seated in the header avatar. The seat is the intended fallback; its keyboard consequence is what fails the row. Owner decision: keep the seat plus bring-back tab as the keyboard route, or keep him visible for keyboard users.

### F5. Calm and reduced motion: celebrating moves him 22 px sideways in one step (A02, desktop and phone)

- **Repro:** Phase 3 quiz with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (or Calm on). Call `studigoCompanion.event("setDone")` and watch the `.cmpWin` transform.
- **Expected:** no hops in Calm. The Calm text in Room Settings reads "No hops, spins or sparkles", and A02 expects no autonomous movement.
- **Actual:** `.cmpWin` shifts by 22 px times the size scale in one step, with no transition. Over 37 (desktop) and 38 (phone) samples the largest change was 22 px. Sparks are zero and the body animation is off, so this offset is the only movement.
- **Code:** [companion.css](../../../apps/web/components/companion/companion.css) lines 47 and 48 translate the window for `[data-pose="celebrate"]`. The reduced-motion block (from line 405) turns off transitions and the body animation but not this offset. It is a static step rather than a continuous hop, so the owner decides whether it breaks the Calm promise. The row stays FAIL under the threshold used here (no translate change above 2 px).

## Harness corrections made before the final run

These changed the harness, not the product. The table above comes only from the final full run.

- Touch and keyboard checks compare centres, use the 4 px viewport margin and the safe-edge clamp. The first versions compared edges of a rotated, scaled window and expected unclamped moves.

- Keyboard direction points to the open side, so a window already at an edge is not tested in the wrong direction.
