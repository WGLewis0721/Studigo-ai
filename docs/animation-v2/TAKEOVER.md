# Studigo Living Companion V2: takeover brief (owner, 2026-11-21)

Read with `ASSIGNMENT.md` (phases and gates) and `PROGRESS.md` (state). This file adds the owner's product direction and craft standard. Secrets are never written here: the kie.ai key is provided only as the `KIE_API_KEY` environment variable at run time.

## Product direction
Kids like AI companion apps they can talk to; parents like study apps that don't feel like studying. Make kids want to open the app first: come for Studigo, stay for the homework help. Studigo = AI companion app + Pokémon-style buddy growth + traditional study app.

Three inspirations, three separate jobs (never blend whole designs, never copy their characters, art, sounds or branding):
- **Pokémon GO: buddy growth and customization only.** Progression, growth stages, personalization, unlockable outfits and accessories, persistent identity, growing together over time; growth visible in animation (newer Studigo curious and eager, experienced Studigo more confident gestures). Use the existing growth and outfit systems; no second progression system. Excluded: catching, maps, GPS, AR, PokéStops, gyms, raids, combat, any Pokémon assets.
- **Talking Tom: touchability and responsiveness.** Distinct hit regions (head, belly, other suitable parts) with real gesture recognition: tap, pet/stroke, poke, hold, drag, repeated taps, occasional harmless surprises, talk-to-him with an expressive response. Every interaction: immediate visible acknowledgment, readable facial reaction, coordinated body motion, sound or voice when enabled, natural settle back. Voice input only through existing permissions and speech infrastructure; no open-ended mic monitoring or recording.
- **Coucou: expressive face and micro-emotes.** Fast legible expressions, gaze, small idle behaviors, expressive blinking, emotion tied to real activity, short floating emotes readable at small sizes, coordinated face and sound. Original Studigo art and sounds only (Mochi assets are protected).

Example mapping: student appears (curious, welcoming; perk up, smile), starts talking (attentive; slight lean), thinks (curious concentration; head tilt), correct (proud; bounce, flame perk), incorrect (gentle reassurance; supportive nod), pet (happy closed eyes; nuzzle), poke (surprise; quick recoil, recovery), idle (relaxed curiosity; breathing, blinking), achievement (excited; anticipation, jump, landing), returns (recognition; greeting).

## Craft standard: the 12 principles of animation (Thomas and Johnston, The Illusion of Life, 1981)
Squash and stretch (keep volume and anatomy); anticipation (never delays the immediate acknowledgment of a touch); staging (readable without audio, never covers learning content or controls); pose to pose for character actions (start, anticipation, action, follow-through, settle) and straight-ahead/procedural for breath and flame, never by switching generated portraits; follow-through and overlapping action (eyes, then head, then torso; frills, arms, tail, flame trail); slow in and slow out with per-action curves; arcs; secondary action that supports the emotion; timing per emotion (surprise fast, curiosity measured, happiness quick, sleepy slow); controlled exaggeration (restrained idle); solid drawing (stable head, eyes attached, consistent snout, spike, frills, tail, arms, outfits; restrict large turns rather than swapping art); appeal.
Every major animation keeps a checklist in `ANIMATION_PRINCIPLES.md` with recorded visual evidence: intent, anticipation, squash/stretch, arcs, easing, overlap, secondary motion, timing, exaggeration, stable anatomy, staging, appeal, interruption and recovery, no ghosting, no pose snapping. Automated tests alone do not prove visual quality.

## Order and first deliverable
Recover, preserve, rig, animate, validate, interact, express, grow, integrate, test, reproduce, DONE. First mandatory visual deliverable: a 30-second recording of Studigo idling (breathing, blinking, eye movement, head motion, frill and tail follow-through, no loop seam, no identity change), reviewed against the principles (solid drawing, timing, overlap, arcs, easing, appeal). The behavior library does not start until the idle passes.

Stages after idle: B direct physical interactions with hit regions; C expressions (curious, happy, proud, surprised, thoughtful, sleepy, attentive, playfully annoyed, encouraging, excited) and original emote overlays; D voice and learning reactions through the existing event API (`setState`, `react`, `lookAt`, `returnToIdle`), preserving learning logic, grading and progression; E buddy growth and outfits on the same rig, no punishment for mistakes. One `StudigoCharacter` component backed by the canonical `.riv`; a permanent showroom on the production component including a principles review mode; device and performance QA (never present emulation as a device); clean-checkout reproduction; PROGRESS.md kept current with the fields in the owner's brief; reviewable PR. No merge to main or production deploy without authorization.
