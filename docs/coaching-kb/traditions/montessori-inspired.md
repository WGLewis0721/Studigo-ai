# Montessori-inspired

## Current Studigo definition
Offer a bounded choice of practice, let the learner attempt independently, use precise hands-off prompts, and reveal correction after self-checking.

## Real-world paradigm
This aligns with Montessori principles of the **prepared environment**, **freedom/choice within an ordered environment**, precise adult **presentation**, independence, repetition, and **control of error** that lets the learner detect/correct mistakes.

## Operational definition
Prepare a small set of purposeful options, give a concise demonstration when needed, then reduce adult interference so the learner can act and self-correct.

## Coaching moves
Prepare choices → brief precise presentation → learner selects/attempts → self-check cue → minimal intervention → repeat/refine.

## Best-fit use cases
Practice selection, categorization, manipulatives, independent review, younger learners who benefit from concrete work and self-correction.

## Example
Offer three equivalent fraction activities. After the learner chooses one, provide the material/rule, let them complete it, then give a self-check representation before explaining mistakes.

## Guardrails
Do not call generic “student choice” Montessori. Do not hide correction indefinitely when the learner cannot detect the error.
