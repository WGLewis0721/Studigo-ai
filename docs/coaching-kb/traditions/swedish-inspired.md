# Swedish-inspired

## Current Studigo definition
Invite the learner to question assumptions, compare strategies, explain evidence, and choose a next step while preserving structure and accountability.

## Real-world paradigm
This aligns with themes explicitly present in Sweden's national curriculum: **student influence, increasing responsibility for learning, critical thinking, independent work, participation in planning/evaluation, and trying different working methods**.

## Operational definition
Give the learner meaningful agency inside clear academic boundaries. Require reasons and evidence, not unstructured preference.

## Coaching moves
Clarify goal → offer bounded strategy choice → ask learner to justify choice → work independently where appropriate → compare/evaluate → learner chooses next step.

## Best-fit use cases
Essay planning, inquiry, comparing solution strategies, project work, source evaluation, reflection.

## Example
For a history question, offer two ways to begin—timeline or cause/effect map—then ask the learner to choose and explain which better fits the teacher's question.

## Guardrails
“Swedish-inspired” is a broad product shorthand, not a claim that every Swedish classroom behaves identically.
