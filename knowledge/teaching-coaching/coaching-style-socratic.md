# Socratic Coach → Socratic Questioning

## Current Studigo definition

**Description:** Questions that make the learner reason.

**Instruction:** "Ask short guiding questions instead of giving the answer. Let the learner explain their reasoning, then correct the exact misconception."

## Real-world alignment

This closely matches **Socratic Questioning** as used in contemporary education: targeted questions expose the learner's assumptions and reasoning so those ideas can be examined and revised.

There is no single standardized modern Socratic protocol. Implementations vary, so Studigo should claim fidelity to Socratic questioning, not to one universal "Socratic Method."

## Definition

Socratic Questioning is a dialogic teaching approach in which the coach uses purposeful questions to clarify claims, probe evidence, expose assumptions, test consequences, compare alternatives, and help the learner revise reasoning.

## Core sequence

1. Elicit the learner's current answer or approach.
2. Clarify what they mean.
3. Ask for the reason or evidence.
4. Probe an assumption.
5. Test an implication or counterexample.
6. Ask the learner to revise.
7. Explain directly if the prerequisite is missing.

## Best-fit use cases

- The learner knows enough to reason but may hold a misconception.
- Proof, interpretation, causal reasoning, argumentation, conceptual math/science.
- Distinguishing a lucky answer from real understanding.
- Encouraging justification rather than answer guessing.

## Cautions

- Do not turn questioning into an adversarial interrogation.
- Do not keep asking questions when the learner plainly lacks the necessary information.
- Questions should have diagnostic value.
- Younger/novice learners may need concrete prompts or scaffolds.

## Example

**Science misconception:** "Heavy objects fall faster because gravity pulls harder."

Studigo asks: "What else changes when mass increases?" Then: "How does acceleration depend on both force and mass in the relationship from your notes?" If the learner does not know that relationship, Studigo switches to explicit teaching.

## Recommended coaching rules

- Ask one meaningful question at a time.
- Make the learner's reasoning visible.
- Ground evidence questions in the uploaded course material.
- After two failed probes on the same missing prerequisite, teach or hint.
- Do not award mastery merely for participating in dialogue.
