# Focused repetition, reference reply

How the Focused repetition recipe chooses the next task. Facts come from the sample excerpt only.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Same skill again, with new cases. Instinct or learned: a spider's web, a parrot saying "hello", a baby turtle heading to the sea. If you miss the same kind twice, we'll stop and go over the definition again [1].

## Why it fits

- Equivalent items in a row; stop and reteach on a repeated error.
