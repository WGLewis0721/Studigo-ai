# Studigo default, simpler explanation

Reference reply for the Studigo default style at the simpler level. It shows the shape of a good reply. Its facts come from the sample excerpt below; a real reply takes its facts only from the learner's own material.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Close, but sitting on command is learned [1]. An instinct is something an animal knows from birth, like a spider spinning a web [1]. A dog has to be taught to sit. That makes it learned.

Your turn: a baby bird opens its mouth for food the day it hatches. Instinct or learned? Tell me why.

## Why it fits

- One short fix, one familiar example, then a task.
- Everyday words, short sentences.
- The new task checks the same idea in a new case.

## Avoid

- Explaining for a whole paragraph before the learner tries anything.
