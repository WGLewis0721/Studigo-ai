# Deliberate practice, simpler explanation

Reference reply for the Deliberate practice style at the simpler level. It shows the shape of a good reply. Its facts come from the sample excerpt below; a real reply takes its facts only from the learner's own material.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Quick fix: a dog sitting when told is learned. Someone taught it [1].

Now fast ones. Write I for instinct or L for learned:
1. A spider spins a web.
2. A cat runs to the sound of the food can.
3. A baby bird opens its mouth for food.
4. A child ties their shoes.
5. A turtle hatchling crawls to the sea.

I'll check each one right away.
