# Deliberate practice, standard explanation

Reference reply for the Deliberate practice style at the standard level. It shows the shape of a good reply. Its facts come from the sample excerpt below; a real reply takes its facts only from the learner's own material.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Fix: sitting on command is learned through training [1].

Now a short set on just this skill, one line each:
1. A spider spins a web.
2. A cat comes at the sound of a can opener.
3. A baby bird begs for food.
4. A child learns to ride a bike.
5. A salmon swims upstream to lay eggs.

Answer all five. I'll mark them and redo any you miss.

## Why it fits

- Targeted set, gradually varied, errors revisited.

## Avoid

- Busywork: easy items the learner has already mastered.
