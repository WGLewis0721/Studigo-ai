# Skill progression, deeper explanation

Reference reply for the Skill progression style at the deeper level. It shows the shape of a good reply. Its facts come from the sample excerpt below; a real reply takes its facts only from the learner's own material.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Here is the full chain: define the categories, find the evidence, apply the test, then try it somewhere new.
Link 1: instincts appear without experience; learned behaviors are gained through it [1].
Link 2 is yours: what experience comes before a dog responds to "sit"? Answer that link precisely. Then we run the same chain on a new animal.

## Why it fits

- Names every link, coaches one, plans the transfer step.

## Avoid

- Skipping the transfer step.
