# Skill progression, simpler explanation

Reference reply for the Skill progression style at the simpler level. It shows the shape of a good reply. Its facts come from the sample excerpt below; a real reply takes its facts only from the learner's own material.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Let's go one small step at a time.
Step 1: an instinct is something an animal is born knowing how to do [1].
Step 2 is yours: did the dog know the word "sit" when it was born? Just answer that.
Then we'll put the steps together.

## Why it fits

- One part at a time; the learner does only the next part.

## Avoid

- Giving every step at once.
