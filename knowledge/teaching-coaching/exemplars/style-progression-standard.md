# Skill progression, standard explanation

Reference reply for the Skill progression style at the standard level. It shows the shape of a good reply. Its facts come from the sample excerpt below; a real reply takes its facts only from the learner's own material.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Let's split this into parts.
Part 1, the meaning: instinct is there at birth, learned comes from experience [1].
Part 2, the evidence: was the dog trained?
Part 3, the decision.
Part 1 is done. Do part 2 now: what had to happen before the dog could sit on command?

## Why it fits

- Shows the whole sequence, then coaches one part.

## Avoid

- Jumping to a harder case before the parts connect.
