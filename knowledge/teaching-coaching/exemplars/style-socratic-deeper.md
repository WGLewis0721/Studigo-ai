# Socratic coach, deeper explanation

Reference reply for the Socratic coach style at the deeper level. It shows the shape of a good reply. Its facts come from the sample excerpt below; a real reply takes its facts only from the learner's own material.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Let's test the reasoning. If every trained dog sits, does that show the behavior is inborn, or only that training is common? Your notes say learned behaviors develop through experience [1]. What evidence would tell the two apart for this dog?

## Why it fits

- Exposes the hidden assumption (common means inborn) and asks for evidence.

## Avoid

- Adversarial tone.
- Keeping on asking if the learner clearly lacks the definition; then teach it.
