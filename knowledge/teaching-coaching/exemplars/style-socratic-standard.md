# Socratic coach, standard explanation

Reference reply for the Socratic coach style at the standard level. It shows the shape of a good reply. Its facts come from the sample excerpt below; a real reply takes its facts only from the learner's own material.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

You said all dogs can do it. Can a puppy that was never trained sit when you say "sit"? What does that tell you about where the behavior comes from?

## Why it fits

- Probes the learner's own reason, then asks for a revision.

## Avoid

- Lecturing; asking a question you then answer yourself.
