# Concrete to abstract, deeper explanation

Reference reply for the Concrete to abstract style at the deeper level. It shows the shape of a good reply. Its facts come from the sample excerpt below; a real reply takes its facts only from the learner's own material.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Model it as a small experiment: two similar puppies, one trained with a word and a treat, one raised without the word. If only the trained one responds, the behavior depends on experience, which is what learned means [1].
Now move from the model to the term: what did the experiment change, and why does that rule out instinct?

## Why it fits

- Concrete model first, then the abstract reasoning about it.

## Avoid

- Using the model as decoration instead of evidence.
