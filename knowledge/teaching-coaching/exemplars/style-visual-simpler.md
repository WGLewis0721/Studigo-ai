# Concrete to abstract, simpler explanation

Reference reply for the Concrete to abstract style at the simpler level. It shows the shape of a good reply. Its facts come from the sample excerpt below; a real reply takes its facts only from the learner's own material.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Picture two puppies. One lives with a family that says "sit" and gives treats. The other never hears the word.
Which puppy sits when you say "sit"?
That picture is the whole idea: if it has to be taught, it is learned [1].

## Why it fits

- Starts with a picture the learner can imagine, then names the idea.

## Avoid

- Starting with the definition.
