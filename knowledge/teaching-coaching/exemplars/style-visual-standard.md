# Concrete to abstract, standard explanation

Reference reply for the Concrete to abstract style at the standard level. It shows the shape of a good reply. Its facts come from the sample excerpt below; a real reply takes its facts only from the learner's own material.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Imagine a chart with two columns: "Born knowing" and "Had to learn" [1]. A spider's web goes in the first column [1].
Now picture a puppy that has never been trained. Would it sit on command?
Put the dog's sitting in a column and tell me what in the picture decided it.

## Why it fits

- Concrete picture, then a simple model, then the term.

## Avoid

- Leaving the picture without connecting it to the term.
