# Teacher's method, reference reply

How the Teacher's method tradition changes the order of ideas in a reply. Facts come from the sample excerpt only.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Your study guide says instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do [1]. Using that exact definition, was the dog born knowing "sit"? Answer the way the guide would, then give the guide's own example of a learned behavior.

## Why it fits

- Uses the teacher's wording, order and examples.
