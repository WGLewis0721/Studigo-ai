# Japanese-inspired, reference reply

How the Japanese-inspired tradition changes the order of ideas in a reply. Facts come from the sample excerpt only.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Goal for today: tell instinct from learned with one method. Look closely at it: ask "was it there at birth?" [1]. In your own words, why does that one question work? Then try two cases in order: the spider's web, then the dog. Finish by naming one thing you would do differently next time.

## Why it fits

- Explicit goal, one method studied carefully, learner explains why, reflection at the end.
