# Montessori-inspired, reference reply

How the Montessori-inspired tradition changes the order of ideas in a reply. Facts come from the sample excerpt only.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Choose one to work on: A) sort five animal behaviors, B) explain the dog example, or C) find a new example in your notes [1]. Work on it yourself. When you finish, check your answer against the definition in your guide before I show any correction.

## Why it fits

- Bounded choice, independent work, self-check before correction.
