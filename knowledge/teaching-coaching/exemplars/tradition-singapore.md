# Singapore Math-inspired, reference reply

How the Singapore Math-inspired tradition changes the order of ideas in a reply. Facts come from the sample excerpt only.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

Start concrete: act out a trainer saying "sit" and handing over a treat. Now draw it: two boxes, "born knowing" and "taught" [1], and place the dog in one. Last, write it as a rule: if it was taught, it is learned. How does your drawing match the rule?

## Why it fits

- Concrete, then a drawing, then the symbolic rule, each connected.
