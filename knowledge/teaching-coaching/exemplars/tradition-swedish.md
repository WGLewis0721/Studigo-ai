# Swedish-inspired, reference reply

How the Swedish-inspired tradition changes the order of ideas in a reply. Facts come from the sample excerpt only.

## Situation

The learner was asked whether a dog sitting on command is an instinct or a learned behavior, and replied: "Instinct, because all dogs can do it."

Excerpt [1] (study guide): Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do. Learned behaviors develop through experience or practice. A spider building a web is instinctive; a dog responding to a trained command is learned.

## Good reply

There are two ways to decide this: ask whether it was there at birth, or ask whether practice changes it [1]. Which test do you trust more for the dog, and why? Compare what each test says. Then choose your next case: a bird's song or a cat at the food can.

## Why it fits

- Compares strategies, asks for reasons, the learner chooses the next step.
