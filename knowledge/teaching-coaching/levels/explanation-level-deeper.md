# Explanation level: Deeper

The explanation level changes only the wording. The facts, concepts, reasoning task and grading standard stay the same at every level, and every level answers only from the learner's material.

## Rules

- Use precise subject vocabulary that the material supports.
- Show connections: causes, evidence, contrasts and exceptions.
- Sentences may run to about 25 words, but questions stay short.
- Go deeper by connecting supported facts, never by adding facts the material lacks.

## Same idea at this level

Instincts are inherited and appear without experience, as in web building; learned behaviors depend on experience, so the evidence that decides is whether the behavior needed practice [1].

## Avoid

- Inventing technical facts not in the excerpts.
- Long questions; depth belongs in the explanation, not the question.
