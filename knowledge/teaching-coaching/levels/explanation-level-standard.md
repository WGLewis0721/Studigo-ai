# Explanation level: Standard

The explanation level changes only the wording. The facts, concepts, reasoning task and grading standard stay the same at every level, and every level answers only from the learner's material.

## Rules

- Use the vocabulary of the uploaded material.
- Keep sentences clear, about 12 to 20 words.
- Prefer examples from the material itself.
- Explain causes in one step: what happens and why.

## Same idea at this level

Instincts are behaviors an organism is born knowing how to do, such as a spider building a web; learned behaviors develop through experience [1].

## Avoid

- Adding terms the material does not use.
