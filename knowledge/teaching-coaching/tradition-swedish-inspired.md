# Swedish-Inspired → Learner Agency + Critical Inquiry

## Current Studigo definition

**Description:** Curiosity, independence, critical thinking.

**Instruction:** "Use a Swedish-inspired approach: invite the learner to question assumptions, compare strategies, explain evidence, and choose a next step. Preserve structure and accountability while giving the learner meaningful agency."

## Real-world alignment

There is no single canonical "Swedish method" matching this UI option.

The strongest defensible alignment is to **values explicitly present in Sweden's national curriculum (Lgr22)**:

- pupils should have real influence and take responsibility;
- schools should develop the ability to critically examine information, facts, and circumstances;
- schools should stimulate creativity, curiosity, and self-confidence;
- pupils should have opportunities to take initiative and work independently and with others.

Accordingly this record is a **curriculum-values composite**, not a claim that every Swedish classroom follows one pedagogy.

## Definition

The coach maintains clear learning goals while giving the learner meaningful choices in approach, requiring reasons/evidence, encouraging comparison of alternatives, and building responsibility for the next step.

## Core sequence

1. State the learning objective and constraints.
2. Ask what the learner thinks or which approach they would choose.
3. Require evidence or reasoning.
4. Compare alternatives.
5. Let the learner choose among valid next steps.
6. Hold the learner accountable for completing and evaluating that choice.
7. Intervene directly when prerequisite knowledge is missing.

## Best-fit use cases

- Open-ended questions.
- Comparing strategies.
- Source evaluation.
- Writing and argumentation.
- Reflective planning.
- Learners ready to take more control of the process.

## Cautions

- Agency without structure can become aimlessness.
- Choice should be bounded by the teacher's scope and the learner's readiness.
- Do not turn national curriculum values into a stereotype about an entire country's classroom practice.
- When the learner is a novice, explicit instruction may be needed before meaningful strategic choice is possible.

## Example

A learner must explain causes of an event from class material. Studigo offers two valid ways to organize the response—chronological or cause/effect—asks which the learner prefers, then requires evidence from the source and a comparison of what each organization makes easier or harder to show.

## Recommended coaching rules

- Offer meaningful, bounded choices.
- Ask for reasons before affirming a strategy.
- Make the learner evaluate the result of their chosen approach.
- Preserve explicit goals and deadlines.
- Use direct teaching when agency would merely force guessing.
