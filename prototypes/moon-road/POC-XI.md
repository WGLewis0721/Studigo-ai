# POC XI — Moon Keep with the Moon Library (golden image)

**Current golden image** (tag `golden/poc-xi-moon-keep`, approved by William 2026-09-30). It supersedes POC X, which stays playable and frozen at `dist/poc-x/`. `dist/poc-xi/` is POC X plus one new room, **T Moon Library**, used to prove the LDtk → TexturePacker → Phaser pipeline from [TOOLCHAIN.md](TOOLCHAIN.md).

- Route: `dist/poc-xi/`. Not linked from the Studigo app.
- Entry: east door of the Trine Guardian room (map 8,1), behind a `3 · 4` seal (×3 or ×4 beam). Beat the Trine Guardian first.
- Contents: a save shrine (×2/×3 refills), skeleton `2 · 3`, skeleton `4 · 1`, bat `1 · 4`, and a `12` stone block (×2, ×3 or ×4) over **energy tank 5** (+1 max heart). Map is now 19 rooms, 10 collectibles.

## How the room is made

| Step | Source | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Layout and entities | `art-source/poc-xi/moon-library.ldtk` (edit in LDtk) | |
| Export | `node scripts/ldtk-export.mjs` | `dist/poc-xi/levels.js` (generated; a test fails if it drifts from the .ldtk) |
| Props (shelf, lantern, lectern, books) | `scripts/make-library-props.py` -> `art-source/poc-xi/frames/*.png` (polish in Pixelorama) | |
| Atlas | `node scripts/pack-atlas.mjs library` (TexturePacker, Phaser format) | `dist/poc-xi/art/library.png/.json` |

LDtk conventions: IntGrid `Collision` 1 = wall, 2 = one-way platform. Skeleton/Item/Shrine sit in the open cell above the floor; Bat sits where it hangs; Block size is its footprint; Prop anchor is bottom-left in pixels.

## Status and caveats

- Tests: `node --test "test/**/*.test.mjs"` (113 pass, includes world graph, solver and .ldtk/levels.js sync).
- Rendered and checked in the browser; not yet playtested end to end by hand.
- The `.ldtk` was generated by script, not first authored in LDtk. LDtk opens it, but re-saving in LDtk may reformat the JSON (the export still works).
- TexturePacker is a licensed local tool; only its output atlas is committed.

## Polish rounds

**Round 1.** Orange dragon with feet grounded in every frame (`scripts/recolor-dragon.py` -> `art/dragon-orange.png`); the Pendulum dummy keeps one hanging pose; auto-fire stops once in-flight shots already cover the kill (`remainingHp` in `rules.js`) and shots fly at 960 px/s.

**Round 2.** Distinct beam sprites per power (spark bolt, fireball, lance, crescent, star), muzzle flash, impact rings, a Clock Shield bubble with gear and charge pips, and carved-stone numbered blocks with brass studs, a number plaque and three crack stages. Hand-drawn by `scripts/make-poc-xi-fx.py` into `art-source/poc-xi/fx-frames/`, packed with `node scripts/pack-atlas.mjs fx` into `art/fx.png/.json`.

**Round 3.** Distinct world pieces from `scripts/make-poc-xi-world.py` into `art/world.png/.json` (`node scripts/pack-atlas.mjs world`):
- Doors: stone frame with a lintel gear, a blue-lamp portcullis that slides up as you approach, and a red chained, padlocked portcullis while a boss is alive.
- Power seals and Rune Gates: carved slab with the opening beam's emblem and a glow strip in its colour.
- Rune Sanctum: a brass rune altar under each rune, and a stone portal arch with four gem sockets.
- Ammo: capsules and drops carry their beam's look (×2 fireball, ×3 lance, ×4 crescent, ×5 star; refill-all is a gold cross).
- Clockwork Warden walk cycle: `scripts/make-warden-walk.py` rigs the standing sprite (legs lift and plant, torso bobs, arms swing) into `art/clockwarden-walk.png` plus per-frame dial anchors in `art.js`. In `boss.js` it stomps toward the dragon when far, backs off when crowded and paces otherwise, shaking the floor and kicking dust on each footfall. This is a procedural re-pose of the single standing frame, not newly painted art; a hand-painted or AI-generated cycle can replace the sheet without code changes.

**Round 4: bosses move like the monsters.**
- Twin Warden: a four-frame walk cycle (`scripts/make-twin-walk.py` -> `art/twin-walk.png`). The live twin stalks the dragon and keeps its range, the dormant one drifts away, with dust on each footfall. Hops and bone throws are unchanged.
- Trine Guardian: drifts across the arena shaded toward the dragon, leans into its movement, winds up (squash and shake) before a dive, stretches through it and leaves afterimages.
- Clockwork Warden: the walk now lifts only the shin and foot (knee guard stays put), which removes the cut-line artifacts from round 3.

**Round 5: shrines.** A carved plinth with a brass brazier replaces the grey rectangles (`shrine_*` frames in the world atlas). The flame burns bright while the shrine is your respawn point and sits low and cold otherwise. One gem per beam the shrine refills circles above it, in that beam's colour (a plain teal gem for save-only shrines), and touching it plays a flare.

**Round 6: attacks, sound, muzzle and a shared pixel style.**
- Boss attacks each have their own look (art from `scripts/make-poc-xi-boss.py` -> `art/boss.png/.json`): the Twin Warden throws a spinning bone and kicks up dust and a floor crack on its hop landing; the Trine Guardian charges a violet orb at its core, fires fanned arcane shards, and marks its dive with a rune circle on the floor and a crack on impact; the Clockwork Warden vents steam from its fists while winding up, then sends bronze shockwave crests along the floor and spinning gears in an arc, with floor cracks on the slam. The training dummy's chimes are silver rings.
- Each beam has its own voice, synthesized in `scene.js` (`beamSfx` / `beamHitSfx`): ×1 a square blip, ×2 a low fireball whoomp, ×3 a rising lance zip, ×4 a wobbling crescent swoosh, ×5 a four-note sparkle. Impacts have matching voices.
- The muzzle flash is half size and sits on the barrel tip. The tip is measured per dragon frame (`GUN_TIP` in `scene.js`) and shots now leave from it.
- Style pass: hand-built sprites share one outline colour (the plum-black on the accepted generated sprites' edges), hard pixel edges, and a 48-colour master palette sampled from the generated art plus accent ramps for the beam colours (`scripts/pixel_style.py`). The generators snap to it; re-run them to refresh. Motion: the dragon squashes and stretches on jump and landing, breathes at idle, recoils on firing and kicks dust on footfalls; skeletons bob, squash before lunging and stretch when hit.
- Not done: the generated sprite sheets themselves (dragon, skeleton, bat, wardens, guardian) are unchanged, so true frame-by-frame animation polish on them would need new or repainted art.
